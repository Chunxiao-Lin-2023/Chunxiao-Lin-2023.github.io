Dynamically secured scan chains and the GF-Flush attack

DFT'212020 – 2021

Implemented a dynamically secured scan chain and verified a scan-based attack; the follow-up GF(2) algebraic flush attack recovers the dynamic key by solving linear equations over GF(2).

< 7 sto recover a 500-bit key
>4300×faster than SAT-based attacks
  • A Python flow converts bench-format netlists into Verilog and inserts the dynamic encryption logic.
  • The attack's math model was verified against ModelSim simulation.
  • The attack extends to scan chains compressed with multiple-input signature registers (MISRs).
