ML-based handover for LEO non-terrestrial networks

ns-32023

A clustering-based handover and target-satellite selection algorithm for an LEO satellite network, aiming at fewer handovers and better bitrate, delay and jitter.

  • Each step, K-means groups the satellites by position; the user picks the cluster with the highest mean RSRP and keeps its satellite if it is in that cluster, otherwise hands over to the strongest one.
  • An ns-3 NTN setup with 10 ground users and 93 satellite base stations.
