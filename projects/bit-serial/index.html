Bit-serial reservoir neuron

ISQED'222021 – 2022

A low-cost reservoir neuron that hard-wires the fixed, sparse ESN weights into a bit-serial multiplier, with canonical-signed-digit encoding and an optimized tanh.

  • Zero weights and zero bits cost no logic; a known 1-bit operand reduces the multiplier to wiring.
  • Much lower resource utilization than earlier hardware ESNs at comparable accuracy.
