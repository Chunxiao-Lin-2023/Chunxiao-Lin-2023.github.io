Hybrid DSP–CLB reservoir neuron

ICNC'262024 – 2025

Nine DSP slices multiply-accumulate while a CLB adder tree compresses their partial sums, so the DSPs never sit idle; a range-addressable tanh keeps the activation cheap.

0.97 µsper prediction for a full ESN (64, 8, 64)
+25%DSP efficiency vs. the DSP-only MAC (72×72 MVM)
5.7–14.2 dBlower NMSE than the AR predictor
  • Range-addressable LUT tanh: interpolation where tanh bends, ±1 in saturation; the DSP is clock-gated above the threshold and the table BRAM drops by 25%.
  • 13% less processing time than the DSP-only MAC on a 72×72 matrix–vector product.
