          This paper proposes a hybrid DSP-CLB reservoir neuron (MAC_DA) and a range-addressable LUT tanh for high-speed ESN inference. A full ESN predicts in 0.97 us (65.98 MS/s) and beats the AR predictor by 5.7-14.2 dB in NMSE.<br>
          <b>My contributions in this work are:</b><br>
          (1) Designed the MAC_DA neuron: DSP slices multiply while a CLB fast adder tree accumulates, raising DSP efficiency by 25%.<br>
          (2) Designed a range-addressable LUT (RALUT) tanh that interpolates only where the curve bends and clock-gates the DSP elsewhere.<br>
          (3) Evaluated the FPGA ESN on mobile MIMO channel prediction against the AR baseline.
