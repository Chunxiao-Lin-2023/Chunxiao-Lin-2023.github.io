The path goes from the latest real-time ESN demo, through FPGA prototyping of the ASIC RTL, to ASIC design and fabrication in 40 nm.
Challenges and directions:
1.	Choose the model size and the fixed-point design together, to keep good wireless performance within the ASIC area limit.
2.	A PC-chip link through a FIFO-USB protocol with a UM232H USB bridge IC.
3.	Prototype as much of the ASIC as possible on FPGA before tape-out.
